How should the flight list feel?

Insights are the hero. Flights are supporting evidence. How strongly should that come through?

A

Labeled section — "Available Flights"

A clear section heading separates flights from insights. Neutral label, no framing. Flights look the same as today but positioned lower on the page.

B

Framed as evidence — "The flights behind this recommendation"

A section heading that explicitly frames flights as the data that produced the recommendation above. Reinforces the advisor positioning — the insights aren't magic, here's the data.

C

Collapsible — collapsed by default

Flights hidden behind a "See flights (3)" toggle. Users who trust the recommendation don't need to scroll through them. Power users can expand. Bold statement that the decision is the product.
