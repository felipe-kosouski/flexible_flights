Where does the AI route summary live?

The route summary is 2-3 sentences of AI-generated prose synthesizing market conditions. Where does it fit in the new hierarchy?

A

Inside the recommendation card

The prose summary becomes the "why" section of the hero card — sits below the directive and price rarity tags, above the date-range insights. One unified block of intelligence.

B

Between insights and flights

A standalone prose card between the insight block and the flights section. Acts as a transition — "here's the full picture before you look at the flights."

C

Remove it from the hierarchy — show on demand

A small "How we got here" link that expands the route summary inline. Keeps the main flow tight. Users who want the detail can get it without it cluttering the default view.
